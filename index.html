06 — FAQ

Good questions.

What glass is used for railings?

Railing glass is safety glass (tempered, laminated, or both). The right spec depends on where it's installed and local requirements; we recommend it during your free estimate.

Do I need a permit?

Often, depending on your city and scope. We'll tell you what applies to your project.

Standoff vs channel vs cable railings?

Standoff holds glass with visible round hardware, giving a floating look. Channel hides the hardware in a base profile for the cleanest line. Cable uses thin horizontal lines with posts for open views; it needs sturdy end posts.

Fixed panel vs SQ4 roller shower?

Fixed means one stationary pane, walk-in style. Roller means sliding doors, ideal for tubs and narrower openings.

How long does a project take?

It depends on glass and hardware lead time. You get a clear schedule with your estimate.

How do I clean glass?

Use a mild glass cleaner and a squeegee. Avoid abrasives.

What areas do you serve?
